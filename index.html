About Smart Hospital Management System

Smart Hospital is a state-of-the-art multi-specialty healthcare institution dedicated to providing compassionate, evidence-based medical treatment. Led by senior Heart Surgeon Dr. Neha (16 Years Experience), our institution unites 8 world-class specialists including Dr. Shobana (Orthopedic Surgery), Dr. Sruthi (Dermatology & Aesthetics), Dr. Jagadeesh (Gastroenterology), Dr. Jaswanth (Pediatric Cardiology), Dr. Harsha (Pulmonology & Critical Care), Dr. Haasith (Endocrinology & Diabetes), and Dr. Kamalesh (Neurosurgery).

Our Vision

To revolutionize regional healthcare through digital medical record management, minimal wait times, and precision surgical outcomes.

Our Mission

Delivering 24/7 emergency response, transparent treatment plans, affordable diagnostic checkups, and seamless digital portal access.

Accreditation

NABH Accredited Hospital, NABL Certified Diagnostic Labs, and ISO 9001:2026 Quality Healthcare Certified.
